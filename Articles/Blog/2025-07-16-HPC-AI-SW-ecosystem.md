### Author bio


[William F. Godoy](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-2590-5178) is a Senior Computer Scientist in the Computer Science and Mathematics Division at Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL). His interests are in the areas of HPC, programming models, workflows and AI for HPC.
At ORNL, he has contributed to scientific software projects funded by the Exascale Computing Project and ORNL's neutron science facilities. He currently serves as co-PI and/or is funded by projects in the US DOE Advanced Scientific Computing Research (ASCR) targeting AI for HPC software, high-level productivity languages, and the integrated research infrastructure.
Godoy received a PhD in Mechanical Engineering from the University at Buffalo, The State University of New York. He is a 2022 [BSSw Fellowship](https://bssw.io/fellowship) honorable mention, a senior member of IEEE, a member of US-RSE and ACM serving in several technical venues.
